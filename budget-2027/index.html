Sri Lanka Budget 2027 explorer: where the money comes from, where it goes, and who it affects

An interactive guide to the 81st Budget Speech (12 November 2026) and its annexures. The 2027 plan has revenue and grants of Rs 6,180bn, total spending of Rs 7,933bn including the Rs 260bn Ditwah reconstruction supplementary, a deficit of Rs 1,753bn (4.5% of GDP) and interest of Rs 2,643bn.

  • Money flow: revenue, spending and borrowing from Annexures III and IV.
  • Sectors: official 2027 totals for 12 sectors from the Citizens' Budget, plus what the speech announced.
  • Initiatives: 267 initiatives with amounts, places, dates and funders.
  • What's new: the 73 new spending proposals, 84 new capital projects and 29 laws.
  • For me: before-and-after changes by group, with income-tax and subsidy calculators.
  • Where: projects by district and province.
  • What next: dated milestones and targets.

Source: the Budget Speech on treasury.gov.lk. This is an unofficial explainer by AAMA.
