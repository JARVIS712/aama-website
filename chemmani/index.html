Chemmani: what the record shows

Content note: this page covers killings, enforced disappearances and the remains of children. It has no photographs of remains.

Human remains were found in February 2025 at a cemetery in Chemmani, Jaffna. The 2026 excavation report counts at least 574 individuals (news reports give 582 to 584). The Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka found in 2025 a “reasonable likelihood” that the burials were unlawful; the 2026 report says the site resembles formal burial more than a clandestine grave. In November 2026 the Jaffna Magistrate said the court need not rely on that report. Who the dead are, when and how they died, and whether a crime took place are not established.

In 1996, Krishanthi Kumaraswamy, her mother, brother and a family friend were killed; the Supreme Court affirmed the convictions in 2004. One convicted soldier said in 1998 that 300 to 400 more bodies were buried at Chemmani. Fifteen remains were exhumed in 1999.
