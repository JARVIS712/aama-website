What we are asking you to confirm. Two pages of the Budget 2027 explorer contain AAMA's own analysis (not statements from the Budget Speech): the Land lens (which funded projects could change land demand, ranked) and the Market lens (which kinds of listed business the Budget's measures lean towards or against). Neither predicts prices and neither names any company or share. Please review the scoring method, each score and its stated reasoning, and the wording, then mark the sign-off table in §6.

Colombo ↔ Kurunegala gateway — Kadawatha–Mirigama · Pothuhera–Rambukkana High 9/9

Why it matters (as published): Two funded, part-built expressway sections finishing within ~2 years. Land near new interchanges (Mirigama, Pothuhera, Rambukkana) gains commuter, warehousing and roadside-commercial demand.

Scores: Commitment 3 (Funded & under way) · Effect 3 (Direct access or demand shock) · Timing 3 (By 2028) · Window: May 2027 – Sep 2028

3. Part B — Market lens (sector-level only)

3.1 Method

For each sector, list the Budget measures that help (+) or hurt (−) that kind of business. Each driver has a strength (1–3), an evidence grade and a horizon.

Score = Σ direction (±1) × strength × evidence weight, over drivers within the chosen horizon. Weights: A 1 · B 0.6 · C 0.3.

Evidence gradeMeaning
AFunded and dated in the speech
BFunded or tendered, or needs a private/PPP partner not yet chosen
CPolicy intent only

The score is a ranking aid for "policy exposure". It is not a return forecast and does not include valuation, earnings, rates, or anything outside the Budget. Strength ratings are editorial judgement and are the main thing to challenge.
