# Explainers launch post

Image: `docs/social/explainers-post.png` (2160 × 2700, 4:5; posts as 1080 × 1350). Source: `explainers-post.html`.

## Caption (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram)

We've started publishing explainers on aama.lk.

Each one takes a public issue in Sri Lanka and works through the documents behind it. Every figure links to the page it came from, and when sources disagree, we show both figures with their sources.

Two are up now.

Chemmani: what the record shows. The mass grave in Jaffna, from the 1996 murder case to the 2025 and 2026 excavation, built from 36 sources, among them the Supreme Court judgment, the excavation report, the Human Rights Commission's findings and Amnesty International's reports. Each statement is tagged by the kind of source it rests on. Content note: it covers killings, enforced disappearances and the remains of children.

Sri Lanka Budget 2027. Where the money comes from and where it goes, built from the 81st Budget Speech and its annexures, with 267 initiatives you can search and calculators for income tax and subsidies.

Both are free to read, with no sign-in. Tamil and Sinhala versions will follow, and more explainers are being written. If there's an issue you'd like us to work through, write to explainers@aama.lk.
